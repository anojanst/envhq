> **Note:** This is adapted from the Apache Software Foundation's Corporate

> lawyer. Legal review is advisable before this is relied upon for a real
> contribution.

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered
into between EnvHQ ("**We**" or "**Us**") and the corporation identified
below ("**Corporation**"), on behalf of itself and its current and future
Employees.

## Why this exists, separately from the ICLA

An employee's work product is generally owned by their employer, not the
employee personally. That means an individual employee's signature on the
[ICLA](./ICLA.md) does not, by itself, bind their employer — and it is the
employer, not the employee, who actually holds the rights this Agreement
grants. If Your organization's employees will contribute to EnvHQ as part of
their job, this Agreement — signed by someone with authority to bind the
Corporation — is what actually secures the sublicensing right the [Elastic
License 2.0](./LICENSE) otherwise withholds (see [ICLA.md](./ICLA.md) and
[LICENSING.md](./LICENSING.md) for the full rationale, which applies
identically here).

## 1. Definitions

**"You"** or **"Corporation"** means the entity identified in the signature
block below.

**"Contribution"** has the same meaning as in the [ICLA](./ICLA.md): any
original work of authorship, including any modification or addition to an
existing work, intentionally submitted to Us for inclusion in the Project.

**"Employee"** means any employee, contractor, or other individual
authorized by the Corporation to submit Contributions on its behalf, as
listed in **Schedule A** below or subsequently designated in writing.

## 2. Grant of Copyright License

Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Corporation
hereby grants to Us and to recipients of software distributed by Us a
**perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable** copyright
license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display,
publicly perform, **sublicense**, and distribute Contributions submitted by
the Corporation's Employees, and such derivative works.

As with the ICLA, the sublicensing right is the operative clause of this
Agreement and the entire reason it exists. The Corporation retains all
right, title, and interest in and to its Employees' Contributions; this
Agreement is a license, not an assignment.

hereby grants to Us and to recipients of software distributed by Us a
perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable (except as

patent licenses granted to that entity under this Agreement shall terminate
as of the date such litigation is filed.

The Corporation represents that:

1. It is authorized to grant the rights set out in this Agreement, and the

2. Each Employee submitting a Contribution has been notified of, and is
   bound by, the terms of this Agreement, and the Corporation will inform
   its Employees who submit Contributions of the terms of this Agreement.
3. Except as disclosed in a Schedule B (to be attached if applicable), the

   would conflict with the rights granted here.

Contributions are provided on an **"AS IS" BASIS, WITHOUT WARRANTIES OR
CONDITIONS OF ANY KIND**, either express or implied, to the same extent and
on the same terms as under [Section 5 of the ICLA](./ICLA.md#5-no-obligation-no-warranty).
Neither party is obligated to provide support.

## 6. Updates to Schedule A

The Corporation may update Schedule A from time to time by written notice
(including a pull request or email to the maintainer) adding or removing
Employees authorized to submit Contributions on its behalf. Contributions
submitted by an individual before their addition to Schedule A, or after
their removal from it, are not covered by this Agreement and require an
individually signed [ICLA](./ICLA.md) instead.
